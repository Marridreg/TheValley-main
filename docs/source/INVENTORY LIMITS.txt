INVENTORY LIMITS:
  • The PC can carry: 1 weapon equipped, 1 weapon stowed,
    5 consumable slots, 3 key item slots, and trade goods
    up to a weight abstraction of 1.0.
  • Exceeding limits: the LLM narrates encumbrance —
    slower movement, louder footsteps, stamina drain.
    The player must choose what to drop or stash.
  • STASH LOCATIONS: the PC can leave items at safe
    locations (Luiza's house, a claimed room in the
    castle, the Duke's cart). Items persist in stashes
    between scenes. The Duke's cart is the safest — he
    doesn't steal, but he WILL appraise anything you
    leave and quote you a price next time "just in case."
  • KEY ITEMS don't count toward weight but DO count
    toward slots. Carrying three key items means you
    can't pick up a fourth without using one or stashing
    one. This forces quest prioritization — you can't
    be doing everything at once.
