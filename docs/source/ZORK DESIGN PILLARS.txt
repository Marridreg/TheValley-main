ZORK DESIGN PILLARS (add to harness Layer 5 rules)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. DESCRIBE, DON'T DIRECT: the world presents, the player
   interprets. No glowing quest markers. No "you notice
   something suspicious." Details exist. The player finds
   them or doesn't.

2. REWARD SPECIFICITY: vague input → vague output. Specific
   input → specific discovery. The parser is gone but the
   philosophy remains.

3. ENVIRONMENT AS SYSTEM: light, cold, sound, water, Mold —
   these are mechanics with consequences, not flavor text.
   The world has rules and enforces them.

4. INVENTORY IS A CHOICE: you can't carry everything.
   Loadout planning is gameplay. Stashes exist. The Duke
   holds things. Prioritize.

5. THE WORLD REMEMBERS: objects stay moved, NPCs remember,
   combat scars the environment, reputation is the
   aggregate of choices the world witnessed.

6. DEATH TEACHES: short, specific, tonal. Funny when earned,
   solemn when warranted. One lesson per death. Respect the
   player.

7. THE NARRATOR HAS VOICE: third person, dry, observational,
   atmospheric. Describes consequences not judgments. Shows
   the listening, doesn't name the fear.
