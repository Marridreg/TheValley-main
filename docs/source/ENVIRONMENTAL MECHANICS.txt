ENVIRONMENTAL MECHANICS:
  • LIGHT: matters in the castle lower levels, the cave
    church, Miranda's lab, and the Forbidden Woods at
    night. Without a light source, perception checks are
    halved. In total darkness, the LLM describes ONLY
    sound, smell, touch, and the PC's emotional state.
    Visual information is withheld entirely. The player
    navigates blind or finds a torch.
  • COLD: matters near House Beneviento (the fog carries
    it), at the Reservoir (frozen in late timeline), and
    at altitude. PC stamina decays faster in cold.
    Companion daughters CANNOT enter cold zones — this
    is a hard gate, not a soft penalty. Bringing Bela
    to the Reservoir is not "risky," it's "she dies."
  • SOUND: the noise mechanic from the combat encounter
    template — loud actions (gunshots, breaking doors,
    combat) raise miranda_attention and attract enemies.
    The LLM should track noise implicitly. A shotgun
    blast in the castle is HEARD. By everyone. The
    daughters come. Quickly.
  • WATER: Moreau's domain. The Reservoir is traversable
    by boat or along the frozen edges (late timeline).
    Falling in is a stamina emergency + mold_exposure
    spike (the water IS Mold-saturated). Moreau is fast
    in water. The PC is not.
  • THE MOLD: passive, everywhere, invisible. Mold
    exposure ticks up near sources and down elsewhere.
    The player can't SEE it ticking unless they check
    the HUD. The first symptoms (nausea, faint whispers)
    are described by the LLM at the mild threshold
    WITHOUT referencing the mechanic — "your stomach
    turns" not "your mold exposure has reached 0.3."
    Diegetic, not mechanical. Zork didn't tell you your
    lamp battery percentage. It said the lamp was getting
    dim.
