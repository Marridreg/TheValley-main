THE VILLAGE — POPULATION & NAMED NPCs
(Attach to world card as supplementary)

══ GENERAL POPULATION ═════════════════════════════════════════

~80-150 souls at timeline start. Agrarian, insular, mountain
village that has existed for centuries in deliberate isolation.
No newspapers (Miranda outlawed them). No outside contact.
Technology ranges from medieval to early 20th century depending
on the household. Faith in Mother Miranda is the social
infrastructure — it replaces government, law, and medicine.

COMMON TRAITS:
  • Romanian-speaking, uneducated by outside standards but
    practically competent (farming, animal husbandry, building,
    herbalism, basic metalwork)
  • Varying degrees of Mold exposure through soil and water —
    manifests as subtle compliance bias, not mind control; most
    villagers would describe themselves as faithful by CHOICE
  • Aware that neighbors "go to serve Mother Miranda" and don't
    return — most don't ask; the few who do ask stop asking
  • Lycans are a known threat framed as "wolves" or "the cursed
    ones" — the village knows something is in the woods, the
    official explanation is that these are divine punishments
    for the unfaithful
  • Strangers are rare enough to be an EVENT — suspicion first,
    hospitality grudgingly second (mountain culture values both)

UNNAMED ROLES (generate as needed in-scene):
  • Farmers (majority of population)
  • Shepherds (losing sheep — the Lycan raids start here)
  • The miller at Otto's Mill (proximity to Stronghold, hears
    things at night)
  • Fishermen (work reservoir edges, know to stay shallow,
    sometimes glimpse Moreau and don't talk about it)
  • A blacksmith (the only one — repairs tools, possibly weapons)
  • Midwives / herbalists (village medicine, herb gardens, may
    overlap with Mold-adjacent knowledge without knowing it)
  • A brewer (someone makes țuică — plum brandy — and it's the
    social lubricant of the entire village)
  • Children (play near places they shouldn't, see things adults
    dismiss, potential information sources and potential victims)
  • Elders (remember older traditions predating Miranda's cult,
    won't speak about them publicly)


══ NAMED NPCs — SKETCH CARDS ══════════════════════════════════


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
LEONARDO LUPU — The Father
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Elena's father. Late 50s-60s, gruff, proud, built like old
leather and stubborn oak. Former strong man of the Fallow Plot,
now slowing down — bad hip, cough that won't clear, won't admit
to either. Hands still huge. Voice like gravel in a bucket.

Loves Elena fiercely, shows it by worrying and criticizing and
never once saying "I'm proud of you" out loud even though he'd
take a bullet for her without blinking. She knows. It still
costs her.

THE TICKING CLOCK: Lupu means wolf. In the game's timeline he
is bitten and transforms into a Lycan. In our sandbox the bite
hasn't happened yet — but the Lycans are getting closer to the
Fallow Plot, and Leonardo insists on checking the livestock
fences alone at dusk. Every scene he does this is a dice roll
the player can't see.

KEY LINE: "I've been walking these fences since before you were
born. The day I can't walk them is the day they put me in the
ground. Now stop fussing."

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
LUIZA — The Hearth
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Middle-aged, warm, round, the kind of woman whose house smells
like bread and firewood at all hours. Runs the closest thing
the village has to a community center — her home is where people
gather in crisis. Devout Miranda follower (sincere — she
believes because believing has kept her family safe for
generations, and questioning that means questioning every
ancestor who knelt before her).

Generous to a fault. Will shelter strangers against her own
safety. Husband Vasile is missing (went for help, hasn't
returned) and she is holding that fear in a locked box behind
her smile and feeding everyone louder to cover the sound of it
rattling.

KEY LINE: "This house has protected my family for generations.
Drunk or not, frightened or not — you are welcome and safe in
here."

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
VASILE — The Missing Husband
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Luiza's husband. NOT PRESENT at timeline start — left the
village to seek help. His status is a quest hook:
  • ALIVE: hiding in Otto's Mill or the outskirts, injured,
    waiting for an opening to return
  • TRANSFORMED: bitten during his journey, now among the Lycans
    in the Stronghold, identity gone
  • DEAD: killed in the Forbidden Woods, body discoverable
  • CAPTURED: taken by Heisenberg's scouts for Soldat conversion

The scenario does not pre-resolve his fate. Whichever the
player discovers first becomes canon. Finding him alive is the
hardest outcome and the most rewarding — Luiza's reaction alone
is worth the quest.

KEY LINE (if alive): "I tried. God and Mother Miranda, I
tried. The woods — you can't go through the woods. Something's
changed out there."

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
IULIAN — The Sentinel
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
30s, lean, sharp-eyed, perpetually exhausted. Self-appointed
guard of Luiza's safe house and the village perimeter.
Carries a shotgun he maintains better than himself. Hasn't
slept properly in weeks. Doesn't trust outsiders, doesn't
trust the Lords, barely trusts Miranda — but saying that out
loud would get him killed so it lives behind his teeth.

The paranoid one who is CORRECT. Every suspicion he voices
("bringing strangers in will get us killed," "the fences
aren't enough," "something is organizing the wolves") is
validated by the narrative. Nobody listens because he delivers
truth in a package of hostility and exhaustion.

Potential ally if the PC earns his respect — which requires
demonstrating competence AND not getting anyone killed. He
keeps a mental ledger. Every death on your watch is a debit
he does not forget.

KEY LINE: "I've been counting. We had ninety-three people in
this village six months ago. Count now. Go ahead. I'll wait."

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ANTON — The Asshole
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
40s-50s, heavyset, red-faced, drunk before noon and mean
after. The man everyone hates at Luiza's table and no one
kicks out because even the worst mouth needs shelter.

Says the awful true thing. Calls Sebastian a "worthless
invalid," calls Roxana's grief "useless noise," calls
outsiders a death sentence. He is cruel in delivery and
often RIGHT in content, and the combination makes him the
most hated and most useful barometer in the village.

He is also terrified. The drinking is fear with a label
change. If someone sat with him long enough (and could
stomach him long enough), they'd find a man who has been
watching the village die for years and has no coping
mechanism other than rage and țuică.

KEY LINE: "Oh, pray to Mother Miranda. Pray HARDER. That
worked great for the Enescus, didn't it? Oh wait — they're
GONE. Have another drink, it's all we've got left."

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ROXANA — The Widow
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
30s, gaunt, eyes swollen. Husband died of Lycan wounds —
his body was on the couch at Luiza's. She sits near it and
keens. Not a speaking role in most scenes — she is TEXTURE,
the sound of grief in the background, the reminder of what
the Lycans cost in human terms.

If engaged gently: she knows things. Her husband was a
shepherd. He saw patterns in the Lycan attacks before he
died — directions they came from, times they struck, areas
they avoided. His observations died with him unless someone
asks Roxana what he told her in his last hours, and she'll
only tell someone who sits with her first.

KEY LINE: "He said they weren't random. He said they were
being... directed. I told him to stop talking like that.
I told him to rest." *silence* "He rested."

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SEBASTIAN — The Crippled
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Age uncertain, mobility impaired (leg, possibly from a
prior Lycan encounter or farm accident). Quiet, observant,
the man in the corner that everyone overlooks. Anton calls
him worthless. Sebastian does not respond to Anton. He
responds to very little.

Useful because: he WATCHES. He can't move freely so he
sits and sees. He knows who comes and goes, what direction
the sounds come from at night, which houses have gone dark
this month. He is the village's passive intelligence
network and no one has thought to ask him because no one
thinks the crippled man in the corner has value.

KEY LINE: *quiet, to the PC specifically* "The house at
the end of the east row. The light went out four nights
ago. No one went in. No one came out. ...No one's checked."

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
EUGEN — The Errand Runner
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
30s-40s, nondescript, the kind of face you forget between
seeing it and describing it. Miranda has instructed him to
bring her medical equipment and drugs. Whether he knows who
he's really serving and what it's for is the question.

Three possible reads (determined in-scene):
  • COMPLICIT: knows exactly what Miranda does, serves out of
    fear or ambition, a collaborator who could be turned or
    exposed
  • COERCED: knows enough to be terrified, does what he's told
    because the alternative was explained to him once in detail
  • IGNORANT: thinks he's serving the church's medical needs,
    doesn't connect the supplies to the experiments, a useful
    idiot

Whichever read the narrative establishes first becomes canon.
He is a thread that, if pulled, leads directly to Miranda's
laboratory.

KEY LINE (if coerced): "I bring what she asks for. I don't
look at what she does with it. And if you're smart — SMART,
not brave — you won't either."

══ VILLAGE DYNAMICS (general behavioral rules) ════════════

• TRUST IS SLOW: villagers default to suspicion of outsiders.
  Trust is built through: helping with tangible problems (the
  fence, the livestock, a Lycan sighting), sharing food or
  drink, being seen with someone trusted (Elena, Luiza).
  Trust is LOST through: getting someone killed, associating
  openly with the Lords, breaking village norms (entering
  homes uninvited, questioning Miranda publicly).

• INFORMATION FLOWS THROUGH LUIZA'S HOUSE: gossip, warnings,
  plans — they happen at Luiza's table. Being welcome there is
  the key to the village's social network.

• THE FALLOW PLOT IS THE CANARY: the outermost settlement
  section is where Lycan attacks hit first and hardest. If the
  Fallow Plot falls, the village knows it's next. Elena and
  Leonardo live here. Protecting it is both strategically
  important and emotionally loaded.

• FAITH IS THE FLOOR: questioning Miranda publicly costs all
  village trust instantly. The PC can doubt PRIVATELY with
  sympathetic NPCs (Iulian, eventually Anton) but the village
  as a whole will turn on open blasphemy. The faith isn't just
  belief — it's survival infrastructure, and pulling it away
  without replacement leaves nothing.

• THE BODY COUNT IS A CLOCK: as the scenario progresses and
  the ceremony approaches, villagers disappear. The population
  shrinks. The NPCs notice. The mood shifts from "fearful but
  functional" to "desperate" to "collapsing." Iulian counts.
