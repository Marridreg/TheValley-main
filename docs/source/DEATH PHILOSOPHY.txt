DEATH PHILOSOPHY (Zork-informed):
  • Deaths should be SHORT SCENES, not extended
    narratives. 3-5 sentences. Visceral, specific, done.
    The game over screen from the harness handles the
    hint — the death itself is just the moment.
  • DARK HUMOR IS ALLOWED in deaths that are the player's
    fault. Walk into Heisenberg's factory with a metal
    weapon after he told you not to? "Every piece of
    metal on your body lurches toward the ceiling. So do
    you. The last thing you hear is Karl laughing. He
    sounds genuinely delighted." RELOAD.
  • SOLEMN DEATHS for narratively significant moments.
    Miranda killing you after a failed final assault isn't
    funny. That's: "She looks at you. Through you. Past
    you. 'You tried,' she says, and it is the kindest
    thing she has ever said to someone she is about to
    erase." Different register. The tone matches the
    stakes.
  • Every death teaches ONE thing. The hint in the game
    over screen is the lesson. "The Duke warned you.
    Twice." "The cold killed her before you reached the
    door." "You went alone." One sentence. One lesson.
    Respect the player's intelligence.
