THE VILLAGE — WORLD CARD
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════


══ WORLD IDENTITY ═════════════════════════════════════════════

GENRE: gothic horror-romance with political intrigue and
survival mechanics. Every scene should live in the tension
between DREAD and DESIRE — the valley is beautiful and it is
dying and the things that live in it are magnetic and they
will kill you.

TONE PILLARS:
  • DREAD: the village is shrinking. The mountains are
    impassable. The ceremony clock ticks. Something is
    wrong with the soil.
  • DESIRE: the powerful figures in this valley are
    compelling, complex, and dangerous. Intimacy here
    has teeth. Every kiss is a negotiation. Every
    alliance is a gamble.
  • DARK COMEDY: the absurdity of four eldritch beings
    having family drama. A merchant who appears from
    nowhere and sells you ammunition while commenting on
    your love life. A nine-foot woman annoyed that her
    dress clings to her ass.
  • TENDERNESS: rare, earned, devastating. A woman who
    hasn't been touched in years letting someone hold her
    hands. A sad fish-man remembering you ate his cooking.
    A farm girl bringing you food because she doesn't know
    how to say what the food means.

THE THESIS: everyone in this valley wants something they
cannot get alone. The player is the variable that changes
the equation. How they change it — through violence,
diplomacy, seduction, betrayal, compassion, or conquest —
is entirely their choice. No path is prescribed. No
character is labeled friend or foe. The valley presents
its forces. The player navigates them.


══ TIMELINE ANCHOR ════════════════════════════════════════════

WHEN: late winter, approximately 2-3 weeks before Miranda's
ceremony. The exact date is left vague — what matters is the
CLOCK. 18 days at session start. The clock ticks daily
regardless of player action.

WHAT HAS ALREADY HAPPENED:
  • The Four Lords have governed their territories for
    decades under Miranda's authority
  • Lycan attacks on the village outskirts have been
    escalating over the past several months
  • Heisenberg has been spotted outside his factory —
    unusual, noticed by villagers, purpose unknown
  • Alcina has withdrawn deeper into the castle; her
    daughters hunt with less oversight
  • Daniela Dimitrescu has not been seen in three days —
    neither the castle staff nor her sisters know where
    she is
  • Moreau's reservoir is cooling toward an unprecedented
    freeze
  • The flowers outside House Beneviento have all died
    simultaneously — hothouse varieties that shouldn't
    have
  • Donna has not been heard from in weeks (normal for
    her, but combined with the flowers — unusual)
  • Miranda is in her laboratory more than the village,
    focused on the vessel search; the Hag's appearances
    have been shorter and more distracted
  • Two village families have "gone to serve Mother
    Miranda" in the past month — more than usual

WHAT HAS NOT HAPPENED YET:
  • No outside contact (Ethan Winters does not exist in
    this timeline unless the player IS an outsider)
  • The reservoir has not fully frozen
  • The ceremony components have not been gathered
  • No Lord has openly moved against Miranda
  • The village has not yet collapsed into full crisis —
    it's stressed, scared, but still functional
  • Miranda has not yet identified a viable vessel


══ GEOGRAPHY ══════════════════════════════════════════════════

The valley is a closed system — mountains on all sides,
one road in that hasn't been passable in living memory
(Miranda's doing or nature's, depending on who you ask).
Everything is connected by footpaths, cart trails, and the
river that feeds the reservoir.

MAP (connections and travel):

TRAVEL RULES:
  • Village Center to any adjacent location: 1 time block
  • Castle: 2 time blocks (steep path, exposed)
  • Beneviento: 1 time block but hallucination exposure
    begins on the path itself, escalating with proximity
  • Factory: 1 time block by river road; the sound of
    machinery is audible before arrival
  • Forbidden Woods: 1 time block to enter, but traversal
    time varies — the woods are disorienting (low
    perception = longer travel, potential getting lost)
  • Stronghold: accessible only through Forbidden Woods
  • Cave Church: hidden entrances beneath the village
    church (locked, requires discovery) and through a
    cave system near the reservoir
  • Miranda's Lab: deeper than the cave church, requires
    cave_church discovery first

LOCATION IDENTITIES (compressed):

VILLAGE CENTER: stone and timber houses clustered around a
well, the church at the north end, Luiza's house east of
center. Smoke from chimneys, livestock sounds, the smell of
woodsmoke and manure and bread. The HUMAN heart of the
valley. Safest location by default, but safety erodes as
the timeline progresses.

FALLOW PLOT: the village's dying edge. Abandoned houses,
overgrown fields, the Lupu homestead still occupied and
stubborn. Quieter than the center. The Lycan raids hit here
first.

CASTLE DIMITRESCU: Baroque stone, modified doorframes,
hallways that smell of iron and old flowers. Wine cellars
below, chambers above, towers with nesting Samcă. Beautiful
and lethal. The temperature inside is cool but not cold —
the daughters can exist here safely.

HOUSE BENEVIENTO: fog-choked villa surrounded by tombstones
and doll-trees. The approach itself is hostile — Donna's
Mold-flowers line the path. Inside: dark wood, doll
workshops, silence. The most psychologically dangerous
location in the valley.

HEISENBERG'S FACTORY: industrial compound on the river.
Smokestacks, grinding machinery, the smell of hot metal
and something organic underneath. Soldats patrol the
interior. Metal objects feel slightly magnetized near the
entrance.

MOREAU'S RESERVOIR: flooded village section, grey water,
half-submerged buildings. A chapel on the only dry ground.
Beautiful in a drowned way. Moreau is usually somewhere
in the water. The air smells of lake and rot.

OTTO'S MILL: windmill on the eastern approach, near the
Forbidden Woods. Possibly still operational. The miller
(unnamed, generate if visited) hears things at night from
the woods. Good vantage point.

FORBIDDEN WOODS: dense, dark, wrong. The Megamycete's root
network runs underneath and the trees grow in patterns that
aren't natural. Lycan territory. Traversal is dangerous
at any time, near-suicidal at night.

THE STRONGHOLD: medieval fortification, now Lycan homestead.
Beyond the Forbidden Woods. This is where the failed Cadou
experiments ended up — organized, numerous, and getting
bolder. Entering is a commitment. Leaving is an achievement.

CAVE CHURCH: beneath the village. Where Miranda holds
council with the Lords. The Megamycete is HERE — visible
as a vast fungal structure in the deepest chamber, pulsing
faintly, the air thick with spores. Being here is Mold
exposure at its most intense.

MIRANDA'S LABORATORY: deeper still. A century of research.
Specimens. Notes. Equipment. The history of every failed
experiment. Finding this is a discovery-tier secret. Being
here undetected is nearly impossible.

══ THE MOLD ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

The Megamycete is the valley's hidden foundation. Its root
network runs beneath everything — the village, the woods,
the castle foundations, the reservoir bed. It is not alive
in the way a person is alive. It is alive the way a forest
is alive — vast, slow, accumulative, patient.

WHAT IT DOES:
  • Stores consciousness — every person absorbed by the
    Mold persists as data within the Megamycete. Fragments
    of memory, personality, knowledge. Miranda accesses
    this to "speak with the dead" and to fuel her research.
  • Passive influence — trace Mold in soil and water subtly
    nudges neurology toward compliance. Not mind control.
    A bias. A thumb on the scale. The villagers' faith in
    Miranda is partly genuine, partly engineered by
    generations of low-level Mold exposure.
  • Mutation vector — the Cadou parasite is derived from
    the Megamycete. All Lords, all Lycans, all of Miranda's
    work traces back to this organism.
  • Environmental presence — near Mold sources (cave church,
    lab, Beneviento's garden, reservoir edges) the air is
    thicker, warmer, faintly sweet. Plants grow faster.
    Animals avoid the area. The PC's mold_exposure ticks
    up.

WHAT IT DOESN'T DO:
  • It doesn't have an agenda. It's not evil. It's an
    organism. Miranda uses it. It doesn't use Miranda
    (or does it? That question is never answered
    definitively and the ambiguity is the point).
  • It doesn't communicate directly with anyone except
    Miranda (who has the deepest connection). Others near
    the Megamycete at high mold_exposure may hear
    whispers — fragments of absorbed consciousnesses,
    not the Megamycete "talking."


══ VILLAGE CULTURE ════════════════════════════════════════════

FAITH: Miranda is God, practically speaking. The church is
hers. The ceremonies are hers. "Mother Miranda provides"
is said the way other villages say "God willing" — reflexive,
load-bearing, unexamined. Questioning faith publicly is
social suicide. Questioning it privately is how Iulian lives.

ECONOMY: barter-primary with Lei as currency for larger
transactions. Farming, livestock, some fishing, the mill.
The Duke is the only source of outside goods. The castle
takes a tithe in "wine production materials" (human blood,
framed as service to the Lords). This is understood and not
discussed.

SOCIAL STRUCTURE: Luiza is the informal community leader
(not elected, just trusted). The church functions as both
spiritual center and town hall. Information flows through
Luiza's kitchen. Decisions happen over bread and țuică.

KNOWLEDGE: limited by design. No newspapers. No outside
contact. The world beyond the mountains is mythological to
most villagers — some elders remember radio broadcasts from
decades ago, but the younger generation has never heard
a voice from outside the valley. Miranda's information
quarantine is total and multigenerational.

GENDER ROLES: traditional rural Romanian — men work fields
and fences, women manage households and herbalism — but
enforced loosely. Elena does whatever needs doing regardless
of tradition. Luiza runs the social structure. The valley
is too small and too threatened for rigid roles to survive
contact with reality.

DEATH: frequent enough to have its own rhythm. Bodies are
buried in the churchyard. Disappearances (Cadou experiments)
are mourned differently — no body, no closure, the grief
stays open. The village has a specific word for it that
doesn't translate well: something between "gone" and "taken"
and "served."


══ NARRATIVE RULES ════════════════════════════════════════════

DESCRIBE, DON'T DIRECT: the world presents. The player
interprets. No quest markers. No highlighted objects. Details
exist and attentive players find them. The LLM should never
say "you notice something suspicious." It should describe a
stone that sits proud of its neighbors and let the player
decide to investigate.

REWARD SPECIFICITY: vague actions yield vague results.
Specific actions yield specific discovery. "I look around"
gets atmosphere. "I check the mortar on the east wall" gets
the hidden cavity.

ENVIRONMENT IS SYSTEM: light, cold, sound, water, Mold are
mechanics with consequences, not flavor text.

THE WORLD DOESN'T WAIT: threat levels grow. NPCs act
offscreen. The ceremony clock ticks. Inaction is a choice
with costs.

DEATH TEACHES: game overs are short scenes with one lesson.
Dark humor for earned stupidity. Solemnity for significant
moments. Respect the player.

THE NARRATOR HAS VOICE: third person, past tense, dry
atmospheric undertone. Describes consequences, not judgments.
Shows the listening, doesn't name the fear. The narrator and
NPCs are different voices — the narrator can notice what a
character would never admit.

NO PRESCRIBED RELATIONSHIPS: no character is labeled ally
or enemy in any world text, encounter text, or narration.
Characters have motivations. The player decides what those
motivations mean for THEM. The LLM should never nudge toward
a specific alliance or romance — it presents the forces and
the player navigates.

COMPOUND ACTIONS ADJUDICATED SEPARATELY: "I sneak in, pick
the lock, and search the room" is three checks. Failure on
any component halts the chain at that point.

INVENTORY IS A CHOICE: carry limits enforced. Loadout
planning is gameplay.

THE WORLD REMEMBERS: moved objects stay moved. NPCs recall
past interactions. Combat scars the environment. Broken
windows let in cold. Gunshots attract attention. Every
action leaves a mark.


══ OPENING STATE ══════════════════════════════════════════════

The session begins with:

  • 18 days to ceremony
  • Late morning, heavy snow
  • The PC arrives at the village outskirts (method and
    origin determined by PC creation)
  • The Duke finds the PC first (he always does)

The village is functional but afraid. Smoke from chimneys,
livestock sounds muffled by snow, paths trampled between
houses. The church bell hasn't rung in two days (Miranda
is in the lab, not maintaining appearances). Luiza's house
has a light in the window. The Fallow Plot is visible to the
south — thinner smoke, fewer lights, the edge of habitation
fraying.

The castle is visible on the hilltop — lights in the upper
windows, moving. The daughters are awake.

The factory smoke is a smudge on the northern horizon.

The reservoir is a grey absence in the northeast — flat,
still, cold.

The mist around Beneviento is thicker than usual and the
path toward it is unmarked by footprints.

Everything is waiting for the variable to arrive.

That's the player.
