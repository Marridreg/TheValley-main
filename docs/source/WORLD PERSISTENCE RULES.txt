WORLD PERSISTENCE RULES:
  • MOVED OBJECTS stay moved. If the PC barricades a door
    with furniture, the furniture is there next time. If
    they break a window in the castle, the cold air is
    NOW IN THAT WING and any daughter in that wing is at
    risk. Actions have physical permanence.
  • NPC MEMORY: characters remember. Alcina remembers
    what you said three scenes ago. Elena remembers you
    helped with the fence. Iulian remembers you got
    someone killed. The psyche system tracks this through
    commitments and appraisal. The LLM should reference
    past interactions unprompted — "You. The one who
    helped with the east fence. ...Sit. Eat."
  • ENVIRONMENTAL SCARRING: combat leaves marks. Blood
    on the floor stays. Broken doors stay broken. Scorch
    marks from Heisenberg's electromagnetic blasts persist.
    The castle accumulates evidence of the PC's passage
    and the daughters NOTICE. "Someone's been in the east
    wing. The door is wrong. ...Bela, did you—" "No."
  • REPUTATION IS MEMORY: the reputation system isn't
    abstract — it's the AGGREGATE of remembered actions.
    +0.1 village trust isn't a number going up, it's
    Luiza saying "you're welcome at my table" instead of
    "who are you and why are you here."
