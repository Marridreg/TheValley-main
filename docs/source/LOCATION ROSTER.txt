LOCATION ROSTER
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

THE MAIN VILLAGE
  The central settlement. Clustered houses, a church (Miranda's
  church), farmland, livestock pens. This is where the surviving
  population lives. Imagine a Romanian mountain hamlet — stone
  foundations, wooden upper floors, thatched or slate roofs, narrow
  muddy paths, a well in the center, smoke from chimneys.
  Population: never given a specific number in-game, but based on
  the number of houses visible, named NPCs, and the "genocide"
  scale described — probably 80-150 people at our timeline start.
  Small enough that everyone knows everyone. Large enough that you
  can't watch all of them.

THE FALLOW PLOT
  An abandoned agricultural section on the outskirts of the main
  village. Elena and Leonardo Lupu's house is near here. The name
  says it all — land that USED to be farmed and isn't anymore.
  Why? Population decline from Miranda's experiments thinning the
  workforce over decades. In our timeline this area is partially
  inhabited (the Lupus live nearby) but visibly declining — empty
  houses, overgrown fields, the feeling of a settlement shrinking
  inward. THIS is likely your "other village" — not a separate
  settlement but a district of the village that's been slowly
  abandoned as Miranda's experiments consumed its residents.
  In our sandbox: some families still cling on here. They're the
  outer ring — most vulnerable to Lycan raids, least protected,
  first to go when things escalate.

THE STRONGHOLD
  Southwest of the village, on the other side of the Forbidden
  Woods. Medieval construction — built as a border station by a
  kingdom on the other side of the mountains. By our timeline it's
  been taken over by Lycans as their homestead. This is where the
  failed Cadou experiments ended up — cast out of the village but
  not far enough. The Stronghold is essentially a Lycan fortress
  now, and the fact that it's THIS close to the village is a
  perpetual threat.
  In our sandbox: the Lycans are getting bolder. They've been
  contained to the Stronghold and Forbidden Woods for years but
  the boundary is eroding. Sheep go missing. Then dogs. Then
  shepherds.

CASTLE DIMITRESCU
  On the hill overlooking the village. Alcina's domain. Wine
  cellars, chambers, towers. The castle predates Miranda — it was
  Alcina's family estate. Connected to the village by a path but
  socially a separate world.

HOUSE BENEVIENTO
  Donna's villa. Isolated, surrounded by mist and dead gardens.
  The approach itself is a psychological hazard — hallucinations
  begin before you reach the front door.

MOREAU'S RESERVOIR
  The flooded village section where Salvatore lives. In our
  timeline: the reservoir hasn't frozen over yet (that's a sign
  of escalation closer to the game's events). Moreau patrols the
  water. Fishermen from the village sometimes work the edges and
  know to stay in the shallows.

HEISENBERG'S FACTORY
  Industrial compound in the mountains. Powered by the river.
  Karl's domain — full of half-finished Soldats, machinery, and
  the sound of metal. Villagers stay away. The smoke from the
  chimneys is visible from the village and smells wrong.

OTTO'S MILL
  An old windmill near the Stronghold approach. Possibly still
  operational in our timeline — a grain mill serving the village.
  The miller would be a useful NPC (information, supplies,
  proximity to the Lycan border).

THE FORBIDDEN WOODS
  Dense, dark forest between the village and the Stronghold.
  Lycans roam here. Villagers who enter don't always come back.
  The wood itself feels wrong — the Megamycete's root network
  runs beneath it and the trees grow in patterns that aren't
  quite natural.

THE CEREMONY SITE
  The altar area where Miranda's ritual will eventually take
  place. In our timeline: empty, waiting, maintained by Miranda
  alone. Visiting it is technically possible but deeply unwise —
  it's the heart of the Megamycete's surface manifestation.

THE CAVE CHURCH
  Beneath the village — where Miranda holds meetings with the
  Four Lords. The Megamycete is down here. The air smells of
  fungus and old stone and something alive that shouldn't be.

MIRANDA'S LABORATORY
  Hidden in the caverns beneath the village. Decades of
  research. Specimens. Notes. The history of every failed
  experiment. If anyone found this, the entire power structure
  of the village would shatter.
